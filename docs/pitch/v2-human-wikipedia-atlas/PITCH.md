# Tymio v2 Pitch

## Hero
> Every non-trivial project has "that one person" - the human Wikipedia - whom everyone asks because the docs, the tickets, and the wiki are all wrong, stale, or fragmented. When that person leaves, the project goes dark for weeks. Tymio is that person, for every project, available to every human and every AI agent, and it never gets tired, sick, or leaves.

![Tymio Human Wikipedia Infographic](./tymio-human-wikipedia-infographic.png)

## One-Line Thesis
Tymio is the living project atlas and the human Wikipedia for a project: the place where humans define the why, AI keeps the rest continuously current, and anyone can ask, "What is going on here?" and get the right answer.

## Why Now
Software teams are entering a new phase:

- Human project knowledge is fragmented across tickets, docs, chat, PRs, and heads.
- AI coding agents are powerful, but they still hallucinate because they lack live project context.
- Project and product management are about to change as much as software development itself.
- The smaller the company, the more painful this is because the same people carry strategy, delivery, and memory at once.

The old stack assumes humans will manually maintain project truth. They do not. The result is stale docs, status theater, and tribal knowledge bottlenecks.

## The Core Insight
There is always a boundary between human judgment and AI execution.

- Humans own the **why**.
- In Tymio, the canonical human-owned "why" is the **Initiative**.
- AI can operationalize everything below that boundary:
  - feature decomposition
  - requirements
  - dependencies
  - risks
  - status
  - documentation
  - delivery memory
  - integration context

This makes Tymio more credible than "AI does strategy" and more valuable than "AI writes code."

## The Product
Tymio is a continuously compiled project graph that connects:

- initiatives and decisions
- features and requirements
- code and pull requests
- integrations and external systems
- risks, dependencies, and status
- generated documents for humans and agents

The main behavior is simple:

## Ask Tymio Anything
Anyone should be able to ask:

- Why did we build this?
- What broke last sprint?
- Which integration owns this flow?
- What is blocking this initiative?
- What changed in the code that affects this requirement?
- What should a new contributor know before touching this area?

Tymio should answer from the live graph, not from stale documentation.

## Product Modes
Tymio can work in two modes.

### 1. Native PM Workspace
For small companies, project management can operate fully in Tymio.

- Initiatives, features, requirements, status, and decisions live directly in the product.
- Tymio becomes the working PM/product system, not just a reporting layer.
- Dynamic Markdown and agent briefs are generated from Tymio, not authored separately.

### 2. Living Truth Layer
For larger teams, Tymio can sit above an existing stack.

- It ingests from project tools, code, and communication streams.
- It compiles one trustworthy atlas from multiple fragmented systems.
- It becomes the shared context surface for humans and AI agents.

## Positioning
Use three layers of language:

- **Founder story:** the human Wikipedia for every project
- **Category:** living project atlas
- **Technical mechanism:** compiled semantic graph with code, integration, and decision bindings

This keeps the pitch emotional, category-creating, and technically defensible at the same time.

## Ideal First Buyer
Working assumption: **founder**.

Why founder first:

- They feel the pain of missing context personally.
- They depend heavily on a few key people.
- They care about both product and delivery, not just one function.
- They can adopt a new system faster than larger PMOs.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a final truth.

## Initial Wedge
The first demo should prove the "human Wikipedia" behavior directly.

Show that a human or an AI agent can ask Tymio anything about a project and get a useful, grounded answer that would otherwise require asking the one overloaded expert.

That is a stronger wedge than "better docs" and broader than "AI coding context."

## Monetization Path
### Stage 1 - The Mind
Sell the live project graph and query surface.

- Per-company subscription
- Freshness and graph integrity SLA
- Human + AI access

### Stage 2 - The Workforce
Sell AI-run project and product operations on top of the graph.

- backlog refinement
- risk detection
- status automation
- dependency tracking
- PMO work reduction

### Stage 3 - The Ledger
Sell autopilot-grade execution based on accumulated decision and outcome data.

- judgment compounds
- delivery becomes more autonomous
- Tymio becomes harder to replace over time

## Why Tymio Can Win
Incumbents mostly manage records and views. Tymio manages meaning.

- Jira, Linear, and similar systems track tasks.
- Tymio models the live relationship between why, work, code, integrations, decisions, and outcomes.
- AI agents need meaning, not just tickets.
- Humans need answers, not status ceremonies.

### Why not Obsidian, Notion, or Confluence
Obsidian, Notion, and Confluence are beautiful vaults a human maintains. They decay the moment people stop maintaining them, and they rely on someone manually linking notes, writing status updates, and keeping documents fresh.

Tymio is continuously compiled from live project signals:

- code, pull requests, and delivery events
- initiatives, features, requirements, and decisions
- integrations and external systems
- risks, dependencies, and outcomes

The atlas stays current even when no one is editing it, and both humans and AI agents can ask it questions directly instead of hunting through wiki pages.

## Strategic Principles
- The initiative is the human-owned why.
- AI should operate everything below that boundary.
- Tymio truth lives in the product; docs are generated from it.
- Integrations are first-class project context, not side metadata.
- Localization matters because CEE/EU buyers are often non-technical and deserve a native-language experience.
